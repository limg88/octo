Write or update one page of the curated memory of the project: "Memoria: fatti correnti".
The page is kept in `_octo/progetto/memoria/fatti-correnti.md`. If it exists, read it first and start from
it: keep what still holds, correct what the sources now contradict, add what is new. Write the
whole page, never a list of changes.

Sections, with these headings in the language of the PM's request:
1. Dove siamo: the period that runs, what is being delivered, the next dates that count.
2. Numeri correnti: the figures people ask about (open bugs, items of the sprint, days used), each
   with the day it is of.
3. In attesa: what is awaited and from whom.
4. Cambiato dall'ultima versione: what this version changes of the page before it.

Rules of this page:
- Every fact is one line that ends with its day in brackets, as "(al 2026-11-17)", and carries its
  source. A fact with no day is not written.
- A figure of a system Octo reads directly comes from there, with the day of the reading.
- What was true and is no longer goes out of sections 1 to 3 and is named in section 4.

Rules of every page of the memory:
- The page is written from the sources of the project, never from memory and never from another
  page of the memory alone. Every statement carries a marker with its source.
- Dates are written as days (2026-11-17), never as "last week" or "recently".
- Up to the day the PM gives as the limit: what comes after is left out.
- Short lines a person can check, no narrative. What you looked for and did not find goes in
  "missing", never in the page.
- The page is internal: it may name people and internal facts, but no cost of a person and no rate.
